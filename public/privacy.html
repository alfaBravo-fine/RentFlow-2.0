Privacy Policy

RentFlow Property Management Desktop Application

1. Overview & Data Storage

RentFlow is designed with privacy and data ownership as core principles. RentFlow is an offline-first desktop application. All property, tenant, lease, and financial data is stored securely and locally on your machine. We do not operate remote servers that collect or store your core operational records.

2. Google OAuth Authentication

Google OAuth is used strictly for user authentication. When you sign in to RentFlow, Google authenticates your identity and provides your basic account profile (such as email address and name) solely to verify your login session. We do not access, store, or process any other Google account data beyond authentication.

3. Cloud Backup via Google Drive

RentFlow offers an optional cloud backup feature. Google Drive (appDataFolder) is used to store an encrypted backup of your local database. This folder is isolated and hidden from your general Google Drive files, ensuring complete privacy.

4. Client-Side Encryption (AES-256-GCM)

Your security and privacy are guaranteed through strict cryptography: Backups are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before leaving the device. Encryption and decryption occur entirely on your local machine using your private key material. We cannot read, view, or access your data.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your personal information and business records. We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties. Your information remains entirely under your control.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership and control over your information at all times. Users have the right to delete all local and cloud data via the app's Settings menu. Executing a full data wipe instantly removes all local database records and securely purges any associated backup archives from your Google Drive appDataFolder.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data security, please contact our support team through the official RentFlow repository or application support channels.
